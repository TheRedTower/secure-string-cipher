# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Secure String Cipher a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive, technically grounded feedback;
- focusing discussion on the work and its effect on users;
- acknowledging mistakes and learning from them; and
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments;
- unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly
representing the project. Project spaces include issues, pull requests, code
reviews, commits, and other project-managed communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report Code of Conduct concerns privately to
<security@avondenecloud.uk>. Do not use a public issue when a report contains
private, sensitive, or security-relevant information.

The maintainer will review reports in good faith, respect the privacy and
safety of reporters where possible, and take action proportionate to the
conduct. Possible responses include a private warning, removal of content,
temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Anyone responsible for enforcement must recuse themselves if they are directly
involved in a report. Because this is a sole-maintainer project, a conflict of
interest may require help from GitHub Support or another neutral third party.

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not tolerated.
Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may themselves be treated as a violation.
